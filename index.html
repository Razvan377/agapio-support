Support

Something not working, or something you'd like to see? This page is the fastest way to reach us.

01

Contact us

Write to us directly and we'll get back to you — bug reports, account issues, billing questions about Agapio Unlimited, or anything else.

rey.rey.agapi@gmail.com
02

Reporting a problem in the app

Agapio has a built-in bug report form — open the You tab and scroll to Report a bug. That's often faster than email, since it reaches us directly with your message.

03

Subscriptions and billing

Agapio Unlimited is billed through your Apple ID, not by us directly — we never see your payment details. To manage, cancel, or request a refund for a subscription, go to your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

04

Your data and privacy

See our Privacy Policy for what Agapio collects and why. You can delete your account at any time from the You tab, under Account.
